# Vehicle Tracker (Android)

A free, offline app to track your vehicles: gas fills and MPG, maintenance and mods, tires and rotations, a service schedule with reminders, documents, and charts. Everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no internet needed.

**This is a beta.** Please report anything odd (see below).

## Install

1. On your Android phone, open this link: **[Download the latest version](https://github.com/hoffjoe-gif/VehicleTracker-Download/releases/latest/download/VehicleTracker.apk)**
2. Open the downloaded file and tap **Install**. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from your browser: tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source**, then go back and tap **Install**.
3. If Play Protect warns that the app is unknown, tap **More details → Install anyway**. (It isn't on the Play Store yet, so Google doesn't recognize it.)

To **update**, download and install the newest version the same way. Your data is kept.

Current version: **2.4.1** (11.05.2026). [All versions](https://github.com/hoffjoe-gif/VehicleTracker-Download/releases)

## Getting started

- Tap **Add Vehicle**, fill in what you know (name is the only must), and save.
- Log a fill from the vehicle's **Gas** tile. Enter the trip miles, gallons and price; the app works out MPG.
- **Service schedule** reminds you when oil, filters, rotations and so on are due, based on your gas fills.
- **Backup**: use **Full backup (.vdtz)** now and then and keep the file somewhere safe (Drive, email to yourself). Uninstalling the app deletes its data.

## Report a problem

In the app: tap **Diagnostic log → Report a problem…**, describe what happened, and send it (text or email) to the person who shared this app with you. Screenshots help.

## Privacy

The app has no account, no ads, no analytics, and does not send your data anywhere. It only shares a file or a report when you tap Share yourself.
